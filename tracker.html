Tracker

Every result moves through five stages. This page shows where each one is, including the ones that failed. It is updated whenever something changes.

The five stages

1. FoundThe system found a candidate and it passed a first rigorous computer check.
2. VerifiedIndependent checkers, a formally verified checker or Lean proof where possible, hostile AI review, a literature check and a 48-hour hold.
3. PublicThe authors of the prior work are told first and given time to respond. Then the note and code go public with a DOI.
4. arXivA written paper on arXiv, ideally with a professional mathematician as co-author.
5. Peer-reviewedAccepted by a journal after independent review.

Separately, a result is marked expert-checked when an independent mathematician has checked it. That can happen at any stage. No result here has it yet.

Withdrawn or not new after all

These looked new at first, and a later check said otherwise. They stay listed because the checking is the point.

  • A claimed 63 for #188 (2 Oct). The search had graded itself with a shortcut; the true figure was about 68. Never announced.
  • Every girth, 5-chromatic, in space. Overtaken by Tomon's paper of 2 October 2026 before release. Dropped.
  • n₄(1/√2) = 13 on spheres was proved by Cabello in 2012.
  • The Lovász-chain interval families on spheres are in Neopryatnaya's 2021 thesis.
  • Most cells of de Grey's conjecture table first counted as new follow from graphs Cherkashin, Kulikov and Raigorodskii had already published.
