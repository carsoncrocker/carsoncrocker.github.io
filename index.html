Erdős Problem #188

published 7 Oct 2026

Color the plane red and blue so that no two red points are exactly 1 apart. How long a run of equally spaced blue points, 1 apart in a straight line, are you forced to have? The answer k was known to be between 6 and 6330.

6 ≤ k ≤ 6330→7 ≤ k ≤ 60
computer-checked proof3 independent checkersformally verified SAT checkernot yet reviewed by a mathematician
Package and note (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23151059)Problem page

Improves the upper bound 6330 of Currier, Mody, Xie and Zhang (arXiv:2606.17194); the lower bound follows the method of Arman and Tsaturian. Gabriel Currier suggested using red cells of different sizes.

Erdős Problem #189

published 7 Oct 2026

Color the plane with two colors. Must there always be a rectangle of area exactly 1 whose four corners share a color? Yes: this shows that 180 specific points already force one. So the fewest colors that can avoid such rectangles is between 3 and 21.

r ≤ 21, no lower bound→3 ≤ r ≤ 21

How to check a result yourself

Download a package from its DOI link, unzip it, and run one command in Linux (or WSL on Windows). It re-checks every step with standard, independent tools and prints PASS or FAIL.

bash verify_all.sh    # Erdős #188
bash verify.sh        # Erdős #189
