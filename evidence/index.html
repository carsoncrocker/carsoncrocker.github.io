Evidence

Every footnote on this site points either to a published paper or package, or to one of the files below. They are short, verbatim excerpts from the project's own records, so you can see what each sentence rests on.

The files

Ledger extractRows of the project ledger, the running record of every claim and check, quoted by line number. Log quotesMy own messages to the AI that are quoted on this site, and lines from the who-did-what timeline. Caught before publishingClaims that did not survive a later check, all caught before anything went public, with the evidence for each. Claude Code usageHow many responses and tokens, measured from the session logs, with the script and its output.

Each page is generated from a plain-text file, which is the source: ledger_extract.md, log_quotes.md, withdrawn.md, usage.md. The usage count comes with its script, measure_usage.py, and its output, usage_output.txt.

How the excerpts were made

  • Excerpts are copied by a script, not retyped, and are verbatim. The ledger is in British spelling and in shorthand; it is left as written.
  • Some text is removed and marked: [redacted: unreleased result] for work that is not public yet, [redacted: private correspondence] for the content of private emails, and [redacted: local path] for file locations on my computer.
  • Snapshots of the AI session logs are kept privately with checksums (ledger row 681). The two published results do not depend on any of this: each package can be checked on its own.

The searches, code and checks behind these results were produced with AI tools (Claude Code, with ChatGPT as a reviewer) inside a process I designed and ran. Corrections are welcome and will be dated.
